Email history is a log of messages Business App sends for your account. Use it to confirm what went out and to open a preview of the original content.

## Access email history

1. Sign in to Business App.
2. Go to `Administration` → `Email history`.
3. Use the Filters panel to narrow the list.

## Filters

The Filters panel includes:

- `Source`: `All`, `Customer Voice`, or `Website`
- `Participants`: `To` or `From`
- `Sent date`: `After` and/or `Before`

## Review the list

The table columns are Timestamp, Subject, To, and From.

Click a row to open a preview dialog. In the dialog you can:

- Switch between `Desktop`, `Tablet`, `Mobile`, and `Html` views
- Review the Subject, To, and From details
- Open the `Events` tab

## Troubleshooting tips

- Cross-check with [Email Configuration](./email-configuration) to ensure SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are still verified.
- If an expected email didn't send, check [Notification Settings](../notification_settings.mdx) and any related automations.
