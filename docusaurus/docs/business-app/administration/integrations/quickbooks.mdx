Connect an existing QuickBooks company to Business App and surface accounting insights in reporting.

:::info Choosing the right QuickBooks connection
There are two connection options:

- **QuickBooks Online**: Sync contacts, companies, and certain financial details, automate review requests from invoiced customers, communicate via Business App, and build campaigns using financial insights.
- **QuickBooks Online: Personal**: Display a high-level financial summary in the Executive Report only. No contact sync or automations are available with this connection.
:::

## Connect QuickBooks

1. Open Business App and go to `Administration` → `Integrations` → `Browse`.
2. Select the appropriate `QuickBooks` card.
3. Click `Connect` and complete any pre-connect form if shown.
4. Sign in to QuickBooks and approve permissions.

## Manage the integration

Use the `Manage` tab on the Integrations page to view status and open settings. When connected, you'll see the established connection with options to reauthorize or disconnect.

## Company sync

QuickBooks does not store companies as separate records. Business App uses the `Company name` on each QuickBooks customer to create and update companies in your CRM.

- If a QuickBooks customer has a company name, Business App creates or updates a CRM company with the name, phone number, and billing address from QuickBooks.
- Contacts with the same company name share one CRM company, and each contact is linked to it.
- If a company can't be synced, the contact still syncs without the company.
- Changes you make to a synced company in the CRM sync back to QuickBooks.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Can someone else connect QuickBooks for me?</summary>

No. You must sign in and complete the connection yourself.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Who can see QuickBooks data after I connect?</summary>

Only you (the person who connected your QuickBooks company) see and interact with the data synced to Business App.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I reauthorize an expired connection?</summary>

Open the QuickBooks integration from `Integrations` → `Manage` and follow the prompts to reconnect.

</details>
