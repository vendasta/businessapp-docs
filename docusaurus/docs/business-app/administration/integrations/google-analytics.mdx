Connect a Google Analytics 4 property to see website analytics in the Executive Report. This integration works with Executive Reports, WordPress Hosting, Advertising Intelligence, Social AI, and CalendarHero. You need a Google Analytics account. Simply sign in to your Google Analytics account to set up the connection.

## Connect Google Analytics 4

1. In Business App, go to `Administration` → `Integrations`.
2. Search for or scroll to the `Google Analytics (GA4)` card and click it.
3. On the integration's details page, click `Add Connection`.
4. Sign in to your Google Analytics account to set up the connection.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>What permissions are required?</summary>

Your Google account must have access to the GA4 property you are linking.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where will I see the data?</summary>

Relevant metrics appear in the Executive Report.

</details>
