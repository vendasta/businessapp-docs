Notification settings in **Business App** control which events send you an email, whether alerts arrive instantly or in a daily digest, and the default for each notification type by product. To change them, go to `Settings` > `Notification Settings`.

Notifications are organized by product. Each section lets you review **Instant Email Notifications** and **Daily Digest Emails** for that product.

## What are the global notification settings? {#global-settings}

At the top of the Notification Settings page, you can configure global defaults that apply to all products unless you override them for a specific product.

- **Instant email and In-App (when available)** — Controls whether real-time notifications are sent via email and appear in-app.
- **Daily Digest** — Controls whether daily summary emails are sent.

Each dropdown offers:

- **Default (Enabled)** — Uses the system default (usually enabled).
- **Enabled** — Always sends this notification type.
- **Disabled** — Turns off this notification type completely.

## What types of notifications are there? {#types-of-notifications}

### Instant email notifications

Instant emails are sent in real time when specific events occur — such as new leads, reviews, or post approvals. They help you stay on top of key activity without waiting for daily summaries.

### Daily digest emails

Digest emails combine multiple events into a single summary message sent once per day. They are ideal if you prefer fewer emails while still staying informed about business activity.

## How do I manage notification settings? {#manage-notification-settings}

To view or adjust your notifications:

1. In **Business App**, go to `Settings` > `Notification Settings`.
2. Expand each product (e.g., Business App, Reputation AI, Social AI, WordPress Hosting).
3. Use the dropdown beside each item to toggle between:
   - **Enabled (Default)** — the notification is turned on by default.
   - **Disabled (Default)** — the notification is turned off by default.
4. Click **Save** to apply changes.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>What are daily digest emails?</summary>

Daily digest emails combine multiple events into a single summary message sent once per day. They suit you if you prefer fewer emails while still staying informed about business activity.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What does Instant email and In-App (when available) mean?</summary>

This global setting controls whether real-time notifications are sent by email and appear in Business App. Instant emails are sent when specific events occur, such as new leads, reviews, or post approvals.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the difference between Default (Enabled) and Enabled?</summary>

`Default (Enabled)` uses the system default, which is usually enabled. `Enabled` always sends that notification type, and `Disabled` turns it off completely.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I turn off the daily digest?</summary>

To turn off all daily digest emails, set `Daily Digest` to `Disabled` in the global settings at the top of the Notification Settings page. To turn off one product's digest emails, expand that product, set each item to `Disabled`, and click `Save`.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I turn off Social AI notifications?</summary>

Go to `Settings` > `Notification Settings`, expand `Social AI`, and use the dropdown beside each notification to set it to `Disabled`. Click `Save` to apply your changes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do global settings override product settings?</summary>

No. Global settings apply to all products unless you override them for a specific product.

</details>
