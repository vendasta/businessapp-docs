The **AI Activity** page shows the work your AI Employees do for your business, so you can see how much they complete each day.

**In this guide, you will learn:**
- How to open the AI Activity page
- What the page shows
- What you see when there is no activity yet

---

## Open the AI Activity page

From the `AI` menu, select `Activity`. The page is available in the web app, not the mobile app.

---

## What the page shows

- **Jobs per day**: A chart of the jobs your AI Employees completed each day, split by AI Employee.
- **Jobs per employee**: The legend under the chart lists the number of jobs each AI Employee completed.

Use the date range picker to choose how many days to view, from 7 to 90 days. The page shows 30 days by default.

The data runs through yesterday and is labeled `Updated daily`. Today's activity appears the next day.

You can only see activity for your own account.

---

## When there is no activity yet

If your AI Employees have no activity yet, the page tells you how many AI Employees are available to you and shows a `Get started` button.

---

## If the report does not load

If the report fails to load, select the retry option to load it again.
