# How do I add a custom domain to my Vibe project? {#custom-domains}

Every Vibe project gets a default published URL, and on the Standard and Pro plans you can also publish it to a domain you own. You add a custom domain in the **Publish** section of your project, and Vibe provisions the SSL certificate automatically.

Country and regional domains like `.co.uk`, `.com.au`, and `.org.uk` are supported. TLS is provisioned automatically for both your default URL and any custom domain, so you don't need to purchase or manage certificates.

## What is my default domain? {#your-default-domain}

Every project includes a default URL that's ready as soon as you publish. You can view and edit this URL in the **Publish** section of your project. No DNS setup is required. It's provisioned and secured automatically.

Visiting the bare domain (without `www`) redirects to the full URL automatically.

## What do I need to use a custom domain? {#requirements-for-custom-domains}

Custom domains require the Standard or Pro plan. On the Free plan, upgrade first through the self-checkout flow in Business App. See [Credits](../credits.md) for information on plan upgrades.

## How do I add a custom domain? {#add-a-custom-domain}

1. Open your Vibe project.
2. Go to **Publish**.
3. Under **Custom Domain**, enter your domain name and click **Add**.
4. Follow the DNS verification steps. Vibe displays the records you need to add with your domain registrar.

5. Once your DNS records propagate and verification completes, Vibe provisions the SSL certificate automatically.

<!-- screenshot needed: DNS verification records panel in Publish workflow -->

Your app becomes available at your custom domain after the certificate is provisioned. If you add a `www` subdomain, Vibe automatically redirects it to your main domain. Country and regional domains (`.co.uk`, `.com.au`, `.org.uk`, and similar) are fully supported — DNS verification, SSL provisioning, and www redirects work the same way as `.com` domains.

## How do I remove a custom domain? {#remove-a-custom-domain}

To remove a custom domain, go to **Publish** and remove it with one click. Your project continues to be available at its default URL.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQs)

<details>
<summary>My custom domain shows "Verifying" for a long time. What should I do?</summary>

DNS propagation can take up to 48 hours depending on your registrar. Make sure the CNAME or A records match exactly what Vibe displayed. If you entered the wrong records, remove the domain and re-add it to get fresh verification instructions.
</details>

<details>
<summary>My custom domain has DNS set up but HTTPS certificate provisioning is stuck. What should I do?</summary>

Remove the custom domain in Vibe, remove the custom DNS records you added for Vibe, then add the custom domain again. This often clears a certificate challenge left over from an older project that used the same domain.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use a custom domain on the Free plan?</summary>

No. Custom domains require the Standard or Pro plan. On the Free plan, upgrade first through the self-checkout flow in Business App.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need to buy an SSL certificate for my custom domain?</summary>

No. Vibe provisions the certificate automatically after your DNS records propagate and verification completes, so you don't need to purchase or manage certificates.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I publish my project first and add a custom domain later?</summary>

Yes. Your project is available at its default URL as soon as you publish. You can add a custom domain later from the **Publish** section of your project.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I change my project's default URL?</summary>

Yes. You can view and edit the default URL in the **Publish** section of your project.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens to my project if I remove the custom domain?</summary>

Your project continues to be available at its default URL.
</details>
