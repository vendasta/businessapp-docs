# Reviews Widget

The Reviews Widget connector guides you through building a review display widget — a carousel, grid, list, or badge that shows reviews for your business. Open it from the Vibe navigation, describe the widget you want, and refine the review source, layout, and filters in the visual editor. The finished widget can be embedded inline in a page within your Vibe project, or exported as a snippet for an external site, without leaving Vibe.

## Describe the widget you want

Enter a natural-language description of the widget in the composer, and it generates a starting configuration for you. For example:

> Show my top 5 Google reviews in a carousel.

## Configure the widget

Use the visual editor to refine the configuration the composer generated, or to build one from scratch.

### Review source

Choose which review source the widget pulls from: Google, Facebook, or your first-party reviews.

### Layout

Choose how reviews are displayed: carousel, grid, list, or badge.

### Filtering

Narrow which reviews appear by minimum star rating, recency, and review source.

## Embed the widget

Once configured, embed the widget inline in a page within your Vibe project, or export it as an embed snippet to paste into an external site.

## When to use it

Reach for the Reviews Widget connector when:

- You want to surface reviews on a page you're building in Vibe, without hand-coding a component.
- You want a review widget for a site outside Vibe and prefer to configure it with AI guidance in the visual editor.
