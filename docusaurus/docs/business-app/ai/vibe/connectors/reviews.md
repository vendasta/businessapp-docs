# Reviews

The Reviews connector lets your Vibe app showcase real customer reviews from your account. Ask for a reviews section, and Vibe renders the reviews your business has actually received — not placeholder quotes.

## What it layers on

Reviews layers on the customer reviews already associated with your account. When you ask Vibe to show reviews, it pulls those real reviews into the generated app — for example, as a testimonials section on a landing page or a reviews widget on your homepage — instead of filling the space with invented quotes.

## Enabling the connector

Open your project's settings and select **Connectors** — see [project settings](../guides/project-settings.md). Toggle **Reviews** on to make it available to the supervisor agent for that project.

## When to use it

Reach for Reviews when:

- You want social proof on your site that comes from real customers, not mocked-up testimonials.
- You're building a landing page or business website and want a reviews section that stays true to what customers actually said.

## Adding reviews to your app

Ask for it directly:

> Add a reviews section to the homepage showing our recent customer reviews.

Vibe renders a reviews section wired to your account's real reviews.

## What you get in the generated app

- A reviews section or widget in your app, populated with your business's real customer reviews.

## Limitations

- The connector displays the reviews your account already has — it isn't where you collect new reviews or respond to existing ones.
