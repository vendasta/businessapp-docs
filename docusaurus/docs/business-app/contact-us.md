## What is Contact Us?

Contact us is a card at the bottom of your Business App navigation. The card shows your service provider's name and `Contact <name>`, where `<name>` is your assigned contact. Clicking it opens a `Contact <provider name>` window with ways to get help. The window can include a message from your service provider, your assigned contact's name and email, a `Send message` button to start a conversation, and a custom support button that links to a support center.

What you see in the window depends on what your service provider has set up. Not every business sees every option.

## Why is Contact Us important?

- Gives you one place to find help without leaving Business App
- Shows your assigned contact's details when your provider makes them available
- Lets you start a conversation with your provider, when that option is turned on
- Can link you straight to a support center with guides and articles, when your provider offers one

## What's included with Contact Us?

- **Message from your provider**: An optional message that appears at the top of the window, above any other options shown
- **Assigned contact**: The name and email of the team member assigned to your account, when your provider shows one
- **`Send message` button**: Starts a conversation with your provider, when enabled
- **Custom support button**: A button, labeled by your service provider, that opens your provider's support center in a new browser tab, when enabled
- **`Close` button**: Closes the window

## How to use Contact Us

1. Look for the card with your service provider's name and `Contact <name>` at the bottom of the left navigation in Business App.
2. Click the card to open the `Contact <provider name>` window.
3. Use whichever options are shown:
   - Read any message from your provider at the top of the window.
   - Review your assigned contact's name and email, if shown.
   - Click `Send message` to start a conversation, if shown.
   - Click the custom support button to open your provider's support resources in a new tab, if shown. Your service provider sets its label.
4. Click `Close` to close the window.

## Additional details

- What appears in the contact window is controlled by your service provider, so the options you see can differ from what other businesses see.
- If your provider hasn't set up anything to show, the contact card doesn't appear in your navigation.
- The support center link always opens in a new browser tab, so Business App stays open in your original tab.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Why don't I see the contact card in my navigation?</summary>

Your service provider controls what appears in the contact window. If they haven't enabled any options, the card doesn't show in your navigation.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why can't I see my assigned contact's information?</summary>

Your service provider controls whether an assigned contact's name and email appear in the contact window. Contact your service provider if you need this information.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why don't I see a `Send message` button?</summary>

The option to start a conversation from the contact window is set by your service provider. If it's off, you can still reply after your provider starts a conversation with you.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where does the support center link take me?</summary>

It opens the support resource your service provider has linked, in a new browser tab. The exact destination and button text depend on what your provider has set up.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I customize what appears in the contact window?</summary>

No. Everything in the contact window, including any message, contact details, chat option, and support button, is configured by your service provider.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is the contact window the same as the Conversations inbox?</summary>

No. The contact window is a quick way to reach your provider or find support resources. Conversations you start from it appear alongside your other messages in [Conversations](./conversations/index.mdx).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does clicking the support center link take me away from Business App?</summary>

No. It opens in a new browser tab, so Business App stays open in your original tab.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Who do I contact if a link in the contact window isn't working?</summary>

Reach out to your service provider. They control the links and content shown in the contact window.
</details>
