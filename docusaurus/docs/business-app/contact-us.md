## What is Contact Us?

Contact us is a card at the bottom of your Business App navigation. Clicking it opens a window with ways to get help — which can include a message from your service provider, your assigned contact's details, a button to start a conversation, and a link to a support center.

What you see in the window depends on what your service provider has set up. Not every business sees every option.

## Why is Contact Us important?

- Gives you one place to find help without leaving Business App
- Shows your assigned contact's details when your provider makes them available
- Lets you start a conversation with your provider, when that option is turned on
- Can link you straight to a support center with guides and articles, when your provider offers one

## What's included with Contact Us?

- **Message from your provider**: An optional message that appears at the top of the window, above any other options shown
- **Assigned contact card**: The name and email of the team member assigned to your account, when your provider shows one
- **Send message button**: Starts a conversation with your provider, when enabled
- **Support center link**: A button that opens your provider's support center in a new browser tab, when enabled

## How to use Contact Us

1. Look for the **Contact us** card at the bottom of the left navigation in Business App.
2. Click the card to open the Contact us window.
3. Use whichever options are shown:
   - Read any message from your provider at the top of the window.
   - Review your assigned contact's name and email, if shown.
   - Click **Send message** to start a conversation, if shown.
   - Click the support button (for example, **Visit support center**) to open your provider's support resources in a new tab, if shown.

## Additional details

- What appears in the Contact us window is controlled by your service provider, so the options you see can differ from what other businesses see.
- If your provider hasn't set up anything to show, the Contact us card doesn't appear in your navigation.
- The support center link always opens in a new browser tab, so Business App stays open in your original tab.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Why don't I see a Contact us card in my navigation?</summary>

Your service provider controls what appears in the Contact us window. If they haven't enabled any options, the card doesn't show in your navigation.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why can't I see my assigned contact's information?</summary>

Your service provider controls whether an assigned contact's name and email appear in the Contact us window. Contact your service provider if you need this information.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why don't I see a "Send message" button?</summary>

The option to start a conversation from the Contact us window is set by your service provider. If it's off, you can still reply after your provider starts a conversation with you.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where does the support center link take me?</summary>

It opens the support resource your service provider has linked, in a new browser tab. The exact destination and button text depend on what your provider has set up.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I customize what appears in the Contact us window?</summary>

No. What shows in the Contact us window — including any message, contact details, chat option, and support center link — is configured by your service provider.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is the Contact us window the same as the Conversations inbox?</summary>

No. The Contact us window is a quick way to reach your provider or find support resources. Conversations you start from it appear alongside your other messages in [Conversations](./conversations/index.mdx).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does clicking the support center link take me away from Business App?</summary>

No. It opens in a new browser tab, so Business App stays open in your original tab.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Who do I contact if a link in the Contact us window isn't working?</summary>

Reach out to your service provider — they control the links and content shown in the Contact us window.
</details>
