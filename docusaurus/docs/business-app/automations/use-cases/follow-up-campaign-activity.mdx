When a contact opens one of your campaign emails, they're signaling interest. Following up quickly, with a personal email or SMS, can turn that interest into a conversation. You can build an automation that triggers the moment a campaign email is opened, so you never miss that window.

:::note
SMS follow-ups require Conversations AI | Pro or Premium. SMS is available only for businesses in the US and Canada.
:::

## When to use this

- A contact opens a promotional email and you want to send a personalized follow-up
- You're running a campaign and want to escalate engaged contacts with a phone call or SMS
- You want to re-engage warm leads automatically without monitoring open rates manually

## How to set it up

1. Create a new automation and set the trigger to `A contact is created or modified`. Set `Action` to `Modified` (the other options are `Created or modified` and `Created`), then choose the field or fields you want to watch in `Contact fields`. If you select more than one field, the automation triggers when at least one of them is modified.
2. (Recommended) Tag the contacts you want to target beforehand. In the trigger, select `Add conditions for starting this automation` and add those tags as conditions, so the automation only runs for the right audience.
3. Add your follow-up action. You have two options:

**Simple follow-up:** Add a `Send a plain text email via Conversations` step to acknowledge their interest and continue the conversation.

**Email or SMS follow-up:** Add an `If/else branch` step that checks which contact details are on file, so each contact gets the follow-up on a channel they can receive:

- **First branch:** The contact has an email address. Add a `Send a plain text email via Conversations` step.
- **Second branch:** The contact has no email address but has a phone number. Add a `Send an SMS message via Conversations` step.
- **Neither:** The contact has no email address or phone number. Add a `Notify a user` step so someone on your team can follow up another way.

When the automation runs, the `Activity` tab shows each step that ran for the contact.
