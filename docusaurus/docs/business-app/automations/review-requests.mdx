Ask customers for a review automatically at the right moment, for example after a job is finished or an appointment ends. Add the `Send a review request` step to an automation, and it sends a review request by SMS or email using your Reputation AI request templates, so you don't have to send each request yourself.

:::note
Sending review requests from an automation requires Reputation AI Premium.
:::

## Before you start

- **Templates:** The step sends your Reputation AI request templates. Each account starts with three request templates: `Review Request` (the default), `NPS Request`, and `Review + NPS`. There's one default email template and one default SMS template. To create or edit templates, see [Templates overview](../../reputation/templates/index.mdx) and [SMS template management](../../reputation/templates/sms-template-management.mdx).
- **SMS:** To send review requests by SMS, set up [SMS review requesting](../../reputation/reviews/sms-review-requesting.mdx) first.
- **Contact details:** A contact needs a phone number to receive SMS requests and an email address to receive email requests.

## Add the step to an automation

1. Go to `Business App` → `Automations`.
2. In `My automations`, select `Create automation`, or open an existing automation. If the automation is live, select `Edit` to make changes.
3. Set the trigger for the moment you want to ask for a review.
4. Add the `Send a review request` step where the request should go in the workflow.
5. Enter a `Step name`, fill in the settings described below, and save the step.
6. Select `Publish` to make your changes live.

## Step settings

### Send via

`Send via` (required) sets how the request is sent, for example `SMS, and if not available, send via email`. When a request can't be sent by SMS, it's sent by email instead. If a contact has no phone number or email address for the channel, the step skips that contact.

### Email template and SMS template

Choose the request template to send in the `Email template` and `SMS template` dropdowns. Both are required when they're shown. The dropdowns you see depend on your `Send via` choice, and each one starts on your account's default template for that channel.

### Schedule review request

`Schedule review request` controls when the request goes out:

- `Use default`: Sends about 15 minutes after the automation is triggered, between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM, on any day of the week.
- `Send immediately`: Sends as soon as the step runs.
- `Delay by`: Waits for the duration you set, then sends within the send window you choose (for example, `Send between 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM`) on the days you select, Monday through Sunday.

### Email follow-up

`Email follow-up` sends a follow-up email if the customer doesn't open the initial link. Choose the follow-up's `Email template` (required), how many days to wait, and the send frequency. Follow-ups are sent by email only, even when the first request goes out by SMS. By default, one follow-up is sent after 3 days.

### Feedback frequency

`Feedback frequency` sets how long to wait before asking a repeat customer for feedback again. The default is 60 days.

### Notes

Use `Notes` to leave details for your team about what the step does. Notes are optional.

## Business address in review request emails

Review request emails show a business address based on your [Business Profile](../administration/business_profile.mdx), unless you've set an override in [Email configuration](../administration/email/email-configuration.mdx#set-a-display-address-for-emails):

- If `Use physical address override` is turned on under `Administration` → `Email configuration` → `Display Address`, review request emails show that override address instead of your Business Profile address.
- If the override is turned off, review request emails show the `Address` from your Business Profile. If your Business Profile's `Service area business` setting is `Hide my address and only show service areas`, they don't show your address. They show your service area instead, or leave out the address section if there's nothing to show.

Updating either setting applies to review requests you send afterward. No extra steps are needed.

## See how your requests perform

Go to `Reputation AI` → `Requests` to see how your review requests perform, including `Requests sent`, `Opened`, `Links clicked`, `Clickthrough rate`, `Conversion rate`, and `Total reviews`. Use the `Source` filter (for example, `Email` or `SMS`) and the `Request type` filter (`Review` or `NPS`) to narrow the results.

## Other ways to send review requests

To send a single request yourself, go to `Reputation AI` → `Reviews` and select `Request` → `Send review request`. To collect reviews from walk-in customers, see [Check-in kiosk](../../reputation/reviews/check-in-kiosk.mdx). For more about review requests in Reputation AI, see [Sending review requests](../../reputation/reviews/reputation-management-premium.mdx).

## Best practices

- **Timing**: Sending requests 1–3 days after a transaction often gets better response rates. Use `Delay by` to wait before sending.
- **Keep it short**: Short templates with a clear call to action usually work best.
- **Don't over-ask**: Use `Feedback frequency` so repeat customers aren't asked too often.
- **Check results**: Review the `Requests` tab regularly and adjust timing or templates as needed.
- **Follow regulations**: Make sure your review requests follow email, SMS, and review solicitation rules in your region.
