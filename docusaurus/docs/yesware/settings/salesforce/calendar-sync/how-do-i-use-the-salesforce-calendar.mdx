To use Yesware's Calendar Sync in O365:
1. Open up a new meeting and click `Invite Attendees`.
2. Invite whomever needs to attend the meeting. 
3. Select the Yesware icon in the Outlook ribbon - this will open up Yesware's sidebar within the meeting invite.
4. As long as `Salesforce Sync` is toggled on, it will include a unique Salesforce sync address in your list of attendees.
5. Send the invite.

This meeting syncs as an open activity to the **first recipient on the invitation with a Contact or Lead record**. It syncs to Salesforce as an Event in the Open Events until the date and time have come and gone, then appears in the Activity History.
Yesware syncs over the title of the event, the location, and the date and time. It does not sync over the body of the invite. All Events that Yesware logs into Salesforce using Calendar Sync will have `[event synced]` added to the Description field of the event in Salesforce. This notation is only added to the Salesforce event and is not included in your O365 Calendar event.
**Please note:** you can only sync a calendar event at the time of the creation of the event; you cannot sync one retroactively or sync an incoming calendar invite.
