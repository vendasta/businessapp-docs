The Monthly Account Activity Report gives you a full picture of how every member of your account is using Yesware in a given month. You can request it yourself from the [Report Downloads](#how-to-request-the-report) page, choose the month you want, and have it emailed to you as a CSV. The report covers each member's usage across emails, templates, campaigns, CRM sync, and meetings.

:::note
The Monthly Account Activity Report is available on the Enterprise and Premium plans.
:::

## What the report includes

- One row per member of your account.
- Monthly feature usage across core areas: emails sent, tracked, and synced; templates inserted and created; campaigns; CRM sync; and meetings.
- Data for the single month you select, from any of the last 13 complete months.

Because the report lists every member on one line across each core feature, it is a quick way to see who is active, who could use enablement, and how your account is adopting Yesware.

## How to request the report

1. Go to the Yesware AppSite and select **Reports** in the left navigation panel.
2. Under **Additional Exports**, click **Report Downloads**.

<div style={{marginBottom: '24px'}}>
<img src={require('./img/monthly-account-activity-report-navigation.png').default} alt="Yesware AppSite Reports navigation with Report Downloads selected under the Additional Exports section" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />
</div>

3. On the **Account Activity** card, open the **Select Month** dropdown and choose the month you want. Any of the last 13 complete months is available.
4. Click **Email Report**.

## How the report is delivered

The report is emailed to you as a CSV attachment from **Yesware Reporting**. The deliverable is the attached file rather than an on-screen view, so check your inbox after clicking **Email Report**.

<div style={{marginBottom: '24px'}}>
<img src={require('./img/monthly-account-activity-report-email-delivery.png').default} alt="Email from Yesware Reporting delivering the account activity report as a CSV attachment" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />
</div>

Open the CSV in any spreadsheet application to filter, sort, or analyze the data. Each row represents one member of your account, with columns for that member's monthly usage, including last activity date; emails sent, tracked, and synced; events synced; templates inserted and created; campaigns; CRM sync; and meetings.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Who can request the Monthly Account Activity Report?</summary>

Account managers on the Enterprise and Premium plans.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which months can I select?</summary>

Any of the last 13 complete months, chosen from the **Select Month** dropdown. Only complete months are offered.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What data is in the report?</summary>

One row per member of your account, with that member's monthly feature usage: emails sent, tracked, and synced; templates inserted and created; campaigns; CRM sync; meetings; and related activity.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How is the report delivered?</summary>

It is emailed to you as a CSV from Yesware Reporting. The report is delivered as the attached file rather than shown on screen.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I open the report in a spreadsheet?</summary>

Yes. The report is a standard CSV file, so you can open it in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet application to filter, sort, and analyze the data.
</details>
