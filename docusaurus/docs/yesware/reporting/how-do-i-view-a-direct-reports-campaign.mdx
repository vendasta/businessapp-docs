# Team Leader Campaign View

If you're a Manager designated as a Team Leader of a team, you can click directly into any campaign owned by a direct report from the [Campaigns by User Report](./what-data-is-shown-on-the-campaigns) to see that campaign's full email touch content and recipient list. This gives you visibility into a representative's messaging and engagement without needing them to share it manually.

## Who can see this

The click-through into a campaign is only available to Managers designated as a Team Leader of a team, and only for campaigns owned by their direct reports. Representatives reviewing their own campaigns, and managers viewing peers or skip-level team members, will not see this option.

Your access depends on how your organization's teams are set up. See [Add members to your team](../teams/#add-members-to-your-team) for how Team Leader roles are assigned.

## How to view a campaign's details

1. Go to the [Yesware Reporting dashboard](https://app.yesware.com/reports/campaigns/#/campaigns/by_user) and select **Reports** in the left navigation panel.
2. Under the **Campaigns** section, click **Campaigns by User**.
3. Select a direct report you manage from the list to open their campaign summary.
4. Click a campaign name to drill into it. This option is only visible to Team Leaders.
5. Review the **Touches** tab for email content, timing, and engagement metrics, or switch to the **Recipients** tab to see everyone enrolled and their current campaign status.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Can I see campaigns for direct reports I do not directly manage?</summary>

No. Access is scoped to your direct reports only. Skip-level or broader team members' campaigns are not accessible through this view.
</details>

<details>
<summary>I manage someone, but I can't see their campaigns. What's wrong?</summary>

Your team hierarchy may not be set up correctly. Contact your organization's Yesware Admin to verify and update team and manager assignments in the platform.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can a direct report tell when I've viewed their campaign?</summary>

No. There is no notification sent to the direct report when a manager reviews their campaign.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I edit or delete a direct report's campaign from this view?</summary>

No. Manager access is read-only. You can view a campaign's touches and recipients, but you cannot modify the campaign.
</details>
