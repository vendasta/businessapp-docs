title: "Reminders"
description: "Set, edit, and manage Reminders in Yesware to follow up on important emails."
---

Reminders let you set a follow-up alert on any sent or incoming email so nothing slips through the cracks. You can view upcoming Reminders in the `Scheduled` tab of the Activity Feed at the top of Gmail.

## Setting a reminder

While composing an email, click the `Remind` button (bell icon) at the bottom of the compose window to set a Reminder. You can also set one retroactively on a sent email, or on an incoming message you're not ready to respond to, by opening the message and clicking `Remind` in the upper right corner.

1. Click `Remind`.
2. Choose whether you want to be reminded only if the recipient doesn't reply, or regardless of whether they reply.
3. Set your date, time, and parameters, or choose a pre-listed option.
4. Click `Confirm`.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Can I change my default Reminder time?</summary>

Yes. On the Reminder setup page, hover over your preferred time and click the `star icon` to save it as your default.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do I view my upcoming Reminders?</summary>

In the `Scheduled` tab of the Activity Feed at the top of Gmail.

</details>
